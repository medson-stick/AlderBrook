The overall concept I intend on making is a replica farming sim similar to Stardew Valley and Fields of Mistria. I want to use this core loop, prototype, etc. for my independent study project next semester where I want to finalize and complete the entire game.

This prototype will contain:
- movement
- farming system
- shop system
- exploring different scenes travelling from area to area (for this, farm -> town -> [if time allows it, fishing beach])
- Add mutated plant chances

I don't want the game to be a 1-to-1 replica, but it will be inspired by the other games. After this semester, I intend on expanding the world, systems, and interactions the player can have.

THIS IS A SOLO PROJECT

Currently in the project there is:
- brown tiles for planting crops and tilling
- shop where if u stand at base of it and press e, it will open shop
- blue bed to right of scene where if u hit e, it will fade out and then in to the next day
